## Network Digital Twin: Concepts and Reference Architecture (IRTF draft)

- **Home**: https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-irtf-nmrg-network-digital-twin-arch/
- **Key concepts**: definition and reference architecture of a network digital twin; benefits and challenges; the distinction between digital model, digital shadow, and digital twin by the direction of data flow
- **Relevance**: frames the broader vision this program serves; we build the twin's data foundation, not the twin. An Internet-Draft of the IRTF Network Management Research Group: work in progress, intended to be informational, not a standard
