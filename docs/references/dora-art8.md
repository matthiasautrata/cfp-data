## DORA — Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, Article 8

- **Home**: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj
- **Key concepts**: identify, classify, and document ICT-supported business functions, information assets, ICT assets, and their dependencies; review at least yearly and on major change; applies from January 2025
- **Relevance**: regulatory driver for function-to-asset dependency mapping and criticality classification (R-7, R-13)
