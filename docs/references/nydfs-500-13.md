## NYDFS 23 NYCRR 500.13 — Asset Inventory

- **Home**: https://www.dfs.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2023/10/rf_fs_2amend23NYCRR500_text_20231101.pdf
- **Key concepts**: written procedures and a maintained asset inventory of information systems; tracked fields include owner, location, classification, support expiration date, recovery objectives; in force since 1 November 2025; subject to annual certification (500.17)
- **Relevance**: regulatory driver for asset attributes and lifecycle dates (R-12)
