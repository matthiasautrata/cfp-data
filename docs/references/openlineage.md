## OpenLineage

- **Home**: https://openlineage.io/
- **Key concepts**: open standard for lineage events: jobs, runs, and the datasets they read and write, with extensible facets
- **Relevance**: inspiration for flow and provenance metadata (layer 2), to be adapted rather than adopted
