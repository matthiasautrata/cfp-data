## YANG 1.1

- **Home**: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7950; encodings RFC 7951 (JSON), RFC 8040 (RESTCONF), RFC 6241 (NETCONF)
- **Key concepts**: modules and revisions; containers, lists with keys, leafrefs, augments, deviations; `config true/false`
- **Relevance**: candidate modelling language for device-shaped data; its datastore model is covered by RFC 8342
- **Used by**: [Idea: model standards landscape](../ideas/model-standards-landscape.md)
